About us

Who we are

A volunteer-run museum preserving Delaware's military past to educate future generations.

The foundation

Volunteers keeping Delaware's history in service.

The Delaware Military Museum is operated by the Delaware Military Heritage and Education Foundation, founded in 2005. Members and volunteers care for the collection, lead programs, and welcome visitors.

The museum sits on First Regiment Road in Wilmington, home of the Delaware National Guard, whose lineage reaches back to 1655. Today the galleries are being rebuilt with new interactive and immersive exhibits; see what's new for the latest.

Our collections

Nine eras of Delaware service.

Original artifacts, photographs, and documents, paired with the stories of the Delawareans who were there, from the first militias to the Guard of today.

  • 1638 – 1775Colonial PeriodNew Sweden, Fort Christina, and the first militias
  • 1775 – 1783American RevolutionThe 1st Delaware Regiment and the famed Blue Hens
  • 1812 – 1815War of 1812Sailors and privateers in the Naval War Room
  • 1861 – 1865Civil WarA border state's divided loyalties
  • 1917 – 1918World War IDoughboys from the First State on the Western Front
  • 1941 – 1945World War IIEvery theater of the war, and Delaware's fallen remembered by name
  • 1950 – 1953Korean WarDelaware's service in the forgotten war
  • 1955 – 1975Vietnam WarField gear, equipment, and the stories behind them
  • 1990 – TodayModern Wars & the National GuardThe Gulf, Iraq, Afghanistan, and the Guard today

The museum also holds a military history library used by casual readers and researchers alike. Research visits are by appointment; contact us to arrange one.

Living history

History you can meet.

Reenacting units bring the collection to life at encampments, presentations, and public programs, portraying the soldiers and sailors of Delaware's past in authentic dress and drill.
