Welcome

The Delaware Military Museum preserves Delaware's military past to educate future generations. Run by the volunteers of the Delaware Military Heritage and Education Foundation since 2005, it tells the First State's story through nine eras of service, from 1638 to the present day.

More about the museum

What's new

New galleries are taking shape.

The Delaware Military Museum is being rebuilt with new interactive and immersive exhibits that bring Delaware's military history to life. Follow our progress as we prepare to welcome visitors into renewed galleries.

Current access

Galleries are closed to walk-in visitors while the exhibits are rebuilt. Group, research, and volunteer visits may be possible by appointment; please call or email first.
