The First State · December 7, 1787

Preserving Delaware's military past, to educate future generations.

From the colonial militias of New Sweden to the Delaware National Guard of today, the Delaware Military Museum tells the story of the Delawareans who served, through artifacts, photographs, and interactive history spanning nine eras of American conflict.

What to See

Nine eras of service, one gallery at a time.

Interactive history boards, photographs, and original artifacts trace Delaware's part in every American conflict, from the colonial period through the modern Middle East.

1638 - 1775 Colonial Period

New Sweden, Fort Christina, and the militias that guarded the Delaware Valley before independence.

 1775 - 1783 American Revolution

The 1st Delaware Regiment, the famed "Blue Hens," and the state that signed first.

 1812 - 1815 Naval War Room of 1812

Delaware's sailors and privateers in the second war for independence.

 1861 - 1865 Civil War Coming Soon

A border state's divided loyalties and the Delawareans who fought to preserve the Union.

 1917 - 1918 World War I

Doughboys from the First State on the Western Front.

 1941 - 1945 World War II

From Fort Miles on the coast to every theater of the war, and the fallen we remember by name.

 1950 - 1953 Korean War

Delaware's service in the forgotten war.

 1955 - 1975 Vietnam War

Artifacts and stories from Vietnam, honored alongside our Virtual Vietnam Wall.

 1990 - Today Modern Wars & the Delaware National Guard

The Gulf, Iraq, Afghanistan, and a Guard whose lineage reaches back to 1655.

Who We Are

Keep the First State's stories in service.

The museum is operated by the Delaware Military Heritage and Education Foundation, founded in 2005 to educate the public, preserve history, and honor those who serve. Members and volunteers keep the doors open, the archives growing, and the presentations coming.
