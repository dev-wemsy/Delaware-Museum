What to see

Walk nine eras of American conflict.

Every gallery pairs original artifacts with interactive history boards and the stories of the Delawareans who were there.

All exhibits
1638-1775 Colonial Period New Sweden, Fort Christina, and the first militias
1775-1783 American Revolution The 1st Delaware Regiment and the famed Blue Hens
1812-1815 Naval War Room of 1812 Sailors and privateers of the second war for independence
Coming soon
1861-1865 Civil War A border state's divided loyalties, opening soon
1917-1918 World War I Doughboys from the First State on the Western Front
1941-1945 World War II Every theater of the war, and Delaware's fallen remembered by name
1950-1953 Korean War Delaware's service in the forgotten war
1955-1975 Vietnam War Honored alongside our Virtual Vietnam Wall
1990-Today Modern Wars & the National Guard The Gulf, Iraq, Afghanistan, and a Guard tracing back to 1655

Who we are

Keep the First State's stories in service.

The museum is operated by the Delaware Military Heritage and Education Foundation, founded in 2005. Members and volunteers keep the doors open, the archives growing, and the presentations coming.
